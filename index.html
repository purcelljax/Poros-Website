Some teams need less.
Some need Salesforce.

This approach is aimed at smaller businesses with clear, manageable workflows. Complex integrations, regulated data, or specialized Salesforce features may make a replacement less practical.

Can everything in Salesforce be moved?

Records and relationships need a migration plan. Automations, integrations, reports, and custom code may need rebuilding. An assessment should identify gaps before you commit.

How would pricing work?

The proposed model is a scoped migration and setup fee, followed by ongoing hosting and support. Actual pricing depends on your requirements and would be agreed after assessment.

What about security and permissions?

Access controls, backups, audit logging, and data handling requirements belong in the design from day one. Requirements must be tested; this draft does not claim Salesforce-equivalent security or certifications.

Who maintains the new CRM?

The support plan should define what the service handles, what your internal owner handles, and how changes are requested. That scope should be settled before launch.
